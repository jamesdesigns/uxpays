## UX/UI Research and Development

### **Goal:**  
To create an **elegant, user-friendly, and intuitive calculator app** that enhances usability while maintaining a sleek and modern design.

---

## **Phase 1: User Research & Market Analysis**  
Before diving into design and development, it's crucial to **understand the target audience** and **identify pain points**.

### **1️⃣ Define the Problem & Goals**  
Many existing calculator apps **lack advanced features and usability improvements**. Key issues include:

- ❌ **No Calculation History** – Users can't track, edit, or reuse previous calculations.
- ❌ **Limited Customization** – No dark mode, theme options, or custom button layouts.
- ❌ **No AI or Smart Input** – Users can't type equations in natural language or use voice input.
- ❌ **No Collaboration or Syncing** – No cloud storage, history-saving, or device syncing.
- ❌ **Lack of Financial & Business Tools** – No tax, loan, currency conversion, or tip calculations.
- ❌ **Poor UI/UX Design** – Cluttered interfaces that are **difficult to navigate**.

### **2️⃣ Features That Set This Calculator Apart**  
✅ **Smart Calculation History** – Store, edit, search, and recall past calculations.  
✅ **AI-Powered Assistant** – Natural language processing & voice input for hands-free calculations.  
✅ **QR Code Payment & Tip Calculator** – Unique feature for **bill splitting and payments**.  
✅ **Customizable UI & Dark Mode** – Users can personalize layouts, themes, and modes.  
✅ **Cloud Sync & Smart Memory** – Store and access past calculations across devices.  
✅ **Scientific & Financial Tools** – Graphing, financial calculations, and engineering tools.  
✅ **Real-Time Collaboration** – Users can share calculations and work on formulas together.
